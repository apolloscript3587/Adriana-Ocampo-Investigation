# Timeline

The timeline separates well-dated events from broad phases for which an exact year has not been confirmed by a strong source.

| Date / period | Event | Evidence / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Childhood | Born in Barranquilla, Colombia; grew up in Argentina; developed an interest in space exploration. | [NASA biography](https://science.nasa.gov/people/adriana-ocampo/). NASA's public page confirms the city and upbringing but does not show a birth date. |
| Teenage years / high school | Family moved to the United States. Ocampo began volunteering at JPL through Space Exploration Post 509 after her junior year in high school. | [NASA biography](https://science.nasa.gov/people/adriana-ocampo/). |
| 1970s–1980s | Worked at JPL while completing university studies; NASA describes her early JPL employment, and UNESCO later summarizes her mission-science coordinator experience. | Exact internal assignment dates are not fully reconstructed here. [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/people/adriana-ocampo/), [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/fr/ethics-science-technology/comest?hl=es-PE). |
| 1983 | Earned a B.S. in Geology from California State University, Los Angeles. | [NASA biography](https://science.nasa.gov/people/adriana-ocampo/). |
| 1992 | Received the Woman of the Year Award in Science from Comisión Femenil, Los Angeles. | [UNOOSA/APOSCOS program PDF](https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/stsc/2022/APSCOSymposiumDuringSTSC-ProgramOutline10Feb2022.pdf); [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/ocampo-adriana-c-1955-planetary-geologist). |
| 1993 | Co-authored the peer-reviewed paper on surface geology and the cenote ring of Chicxulub. | [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00575099). |
| 1996 | JPL Advisory Council for Women Award is listed in biographical reference sources; JPL published a report on radar images that suggested a group of impact craters near Aorounga, Chad, with Ocampo quoted as part of the science team. | Award is secondary-source documented: [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/ocampo-adriana-c-1955-planetary-geologist). Aorounga report: [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/chain-of-impact-craters-suspected-in-spaceborne-radar-images/). |
| 1997 | Received the Science and Technology Award from the Chicano Federation, according to a biographical reference source. | [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/ocampo-adriana-c-1955-planetary-geologist). |
| 2002 | Named among Discover magazine's “50 Most Important Women in Science,” as recorded in a United Nations space-cooperation program. | [Discover feature](https://www.discovermagazine.com/the-50-most-important-women-in-science-5574); [UNOOSA PDF](https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/stsc/2022/APSCOSymposiumDuringSTSC-ProgramOutline10Feb2022.pdf). |
| 2003 | Asteroid later designated 177120 Ocampo Uría was discovered by Marc Buie; subsequently named in Ocampo's honor. | [UNOOSA/APOSCOS program PDF](https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/stsc/2022/APSCOSymposiumDuringSTSC-ProgramOutline10Feb2022.pdf). Exact naming-citation record should still be added from the Minor Planet Center. |
| 2013 | Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam catalog lists the PhD thesis on Chicxulub crater deposits dated 25 March 2013. | [VU Amsterdam thesis record](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/geology-and-emplacement-mechanism-of-chicxulub-crater-deposits-an/). This is the repository date, not necessarily the start date of doctoral study. |
| 2015 | New Horizons performed its first close flyby of Pluto. Ocampo's role is documented as program-executive leadership for New Frontiers. | Mission result: [NASA New Horizons](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/); role: [UNOOSA program PDF](https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/stsc/2022/APSCOSymposiumDuringSTSC-ProgramOutline10Feb2022.pdf). |
| 2016 | Juno entered orbit around Jupiter; Ocampo was named National Hispanic Scientist of the Year by the Museum of Science & Industry in Tampa. | [NASA Juno](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/juno/); [MOSI announcement](https://www.prweb.com/releases/dr_adriana_ocampo_a_leader_on_nasa_s_juno_mission_named_2016_national_hispanic_scientist_of_the_year/prweb13592650.htm). |
| 2019 | New Horizons flew past Arrokoth in the Kuiper Belt. | [NASA New Horizons](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/new-horizons/). Her connection is the mission program-executive role, not a claim of personal spacecraft operations. |
| 2020 | OSIRIS-REx collected a sample from asteroid Bennu. | NASA mission timeline: [OSIRIS-REx](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/osiris-rex/). Ocampo's connection: New Frontiers program leadership. |
| 2022 | NASA en Español profile described Ocampo as a NASA Science Program Manager and reported the Latin America Lifetime Award. | [NASA en Español profile, 29 March 2022](https://ciencia.nasa.gov/gente-de-la-nasa/dra-adriana-ocampo-uria-explorando-el-universo-e-inspirando-en-la-tierra/). This role title is historical, not a current 2026 confirmation. |
| 2023 | OSIRIS-REx returned Bennu material to Earth; VU thesis repository lists her doctoral thesis as a 2013 record. | [NASA mission result](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/osiris-rex/osirisrex-delivers-nasas-first-asteroid-sample-to-earth/); [VU thesis record](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/geology-and-emplacement-mechanism-of-chicxulub-crater-deposits-an/). |
| 2024 | NASA's profile page lists its own last-updated date as 5 November 2024 and continues to title her “Scientist — NASA Headquarters.” | [NASA profile](https://science.nasa.gov/people/adriana-ocampo/). This is important evidence of a lag between the public biography and newer listings. |
| 2025 | Florida state registry records the Stelpolvo Ocampo Uria Foundation, Inc. as filed in July 2025. | [Florida Division of Corporations record](https://search.sunbiz.org/Inquiry/corporationsearch/SearchResultDetail?aggregateId=fornp-f25000003992-365756b8-59a9-44ed-9e78-57f7ec5f6be5&directionType=PreviousList&inquirytype=EntityName&listNameOrder=STELOGISTICSOLUTIONS+L250000482710&searchNameOrder=STELPOLVOOCAMPOURIAFOUNDATION+F250000039920&searchTerm=STELLINK+FLORIDA%2C+LLC). |
| 2026–2027 | UNESCO lists her as Vice-President of COMEST for this term; UCF lists her as Courtesy Research Professor. | [UNESCO COMEST directory, updated 25 Sep 2026](https://www.unesco.org/fr/ethics-science-technology/comest?hl=es-PE); [UCF faculty profile](https://sciences.ucf.edu/physics/person/adriana-c-ocampo-uria/). |

## Chronology caveats

- A reliable year is used when an institution, publisher, or contemporary source provides one.
- A mission milestone is not necessarily the year Ocampo began working on it.
- Awards with only secondary biographical documentation are labeled as such in [Awards and Recognition](awards-and-recognition.md).
- An exact NASA departure date is not established by the sources checked for this dossier.
