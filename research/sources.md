## D. How to interpret this bibliography

- **Highest confidence:** facts that align between current official institution pages and contemporary primary or scientific records.
- **High-to-moderate confidence:** role histories in institutional bios written at the time of the role.
- **Moderate confidence:** older award claims that appear in reliable secondary references but whose original award announcements have not yet been located.
- **Open:** exact departure date from NASA, complete publication list, detailed foundation outputs, and the official minor-planet naming citation.

URLs were checked on 13 October 2026. Some source pages may later change or disappear; this is why access date and source context are recorded.
